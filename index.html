Our Workmanship Warranty — Full Terms

      
Every repair we perform is backed by our workmanship warranty: 12 months or 12,000 miles, whichever comes first, starting from the date of service shown on your invoice.

      
What is covered

      
    
        
  • Our labor and workmanship. If a repair we performed fails because of how the work was done, we will make it right — re-do the workmanship at no labor charge to you.
    • 
      

      
What is not covered

      
    
        
  • Parts, products, and consumables. Parts carry their own manufacturer's warranty, where one exists; we do not warrant parts, fluids, or other products themselves.
    • 
        
  • Parts supplied by the customer.
    • 
        
  • Experimental work. Experimental jobs are performed at the customer's request with no warranty of any kind — not on parts, not on the system or modification, and not on our labor.
    • 
        
  • Damage caused by misuse, abuse, or neglect after the repair — including, for example: driving with no oil or low oil, driving with low or no coolant, continuing to drive after a warning light, gauge, noise, leak, or overheating indicates a problem, overloading, racing, or using the vehicle for purposes it was not designed for.
    • 
        
  • Damage or failure caused by an accident, collision, fire, flood, theft, or vandalism.
    • 
        
  • Failures caused or made worse by repairs, adjustments, or modifications performed by anyone else after our service.
    • 
        
  • Unrelated failures. The warranty covers the specific repair performed, not other parts or systems of the vehicle.
    • 
      

      
How to make a claim

      
    
        
  • Contact us as soon as you notice a problem: call or text 360-772-8205. Stop driving the vehicle if continuing could cause more damage.
    • 
        
  • The vehicle must be made available for our inspection so we can determine whether the failure is covered workmanship. Our remedy under this warranty is repair of the covered workmanship.
    • 
        
  • Inspection visits that turn out not to be covered. A warranty inspection is free when the problem is covered workmanship. If we come out on a warranty claim and our inspection shows the problem is not covered — an unrelated failure, misuse or neglect, damage caused by others, or a part or product failure — the visit is billed as a regular service call at our standard $65 rate, plus our standard diagnostic charge if a diagnosis is performed. By requesting a warranty visit, you agree to these charges if the claim is not covered.
    • 
        
  • This warranty belongs to the customer named on the invoice, for the vehicle serviced, and is not transferable.
    •

Know Before You Buy

      

        
Codes P0A80, P3011–P3024 mean “replace hybrid battery” or a weak block — but a clogged cooling fan or a dying 12V battery can trigger the same warnings. Diagnose first.

        
Core charges explained: every pack carries a refundable core deposit (about $500–$1,350) until your old pack goes back to the supplier. When we install, we handle the core return for you.

        
A straight word on lithium conversions: the much-advertised lithium upgrade kits for Prius were pulled from the market after durability failures — the maker itself now says the project failed, and we will not sell or install them. The upgrade we do stand behind is sodium-ion, and only through suppliers with real backing (Green Bean is owned by LKQ; the Hybrid Geek warranties theirs for 5 years). If anyone tries to sell you a lithium conversion, ask who honors the warranty when they’re gone.

        
Fine print: battery prices, warranties and core charges are set by the seller/supplier and can change. Your pack warranty is with the manufacturer/seller named at purchase; Redemption Mobile Service LLC warrants its installation labor under its standard 12-month / 12,000-mile warranty. Always confirm fitment by VIN before ordering.

The real professional manual: step-by-step repair procedures, wiring diagrams, and service bulletins. Free online with a Multnomah County Library card — and Clackamas County residents can get that card free. On the Vancouver side, Fort Vancouver Regional Libraries carries it too.
