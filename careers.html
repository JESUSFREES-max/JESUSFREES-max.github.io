Work With Redemption — Apprentices & New Grads

Redemption Mobile Service is growing, and we’d rather train people right than hire strangers and hope. Two doors in: start as an apprentice and learn the trade on real jobs, or come in as a new graduate of an automotive program and skip the years of oil changes at the back of a shop. What we look for first isn’t a résumé — it’s character. Honest people with integrity can be taught everything else.

🔧 Door 1 — The Apprenticeship (Start From Zero)

No experience needed. You learn the way this trade has always been learned — next to a working mechanic, on real cars, in real driveways — with a clear path and a skills checklist, not guesswork.

STEP 1
Ride Along
Shadow real jobs — brakes, batteries, diagnostics, roadside calls. These training rides are how you qualify for paid work.
STEP 2
Supervised Work
Your hands on the tools, Denis checking your work. Skills signed off one by one.
STEP 3
Solo Jobs
Run roadside and routine jobs on your own, with backup a phone call away.
STEP 4
Full Tech
Run your own Redemption jobs as an on-call contractor — and get first pick of the bigger work as your rating grows.

How the work is set up: everyone starts as an on-call independent contractor. Jobs are offered to you by text, you take the ones you want, and you’re paid per job — you keep 60% of the labor on every job you run. Training rides qualify you for more kinds of jobs. You pick and choose when you work, and warranty work is never forced on you. Coming soon: loaner truck-and-tool sets for contractors who need a setup — run ours and the split adjusts. You’re responsible for your own taxes and insurance.

What you need: a valid driver’s license, a reliable way to get to jobs within ~30 miles of Clackamas, a phone, and the ability to work outdoors in Oregon weather. Your own basic tools help but aren’t required to start.

🎓 Door 2 — New Graduates & Trained Techs

Just finished an automotive program — community college, trade school, or a shop apprenticeship — and need real-world hours that count? Here you’ll do real diagnostics and real repairs from your first week, mentored by the owner, not parked on the lube rack.

  • Real diagnostic work — full-system scanning, live data, active tests
  • Jobs matched to your level, sent straight to your phone
  • Clear path: start on-call, build your rating, get first pick of more and bigger jobs
  • Hybrid and EV battery work is part of the future here — learn it early

What We Stand On

  • Honesty is the whole business. We quote real prices, we never sell a customer work their car doesn’t need, and if a job is beyond us we say so
  • We warranty our workmanship — 12 months / 12,000 miles — and we stand behind it
  • This is a faith-founded business (Acts 4:12). You don’t have to share the faith — you do have to share the honesty
  • The work is 24/7 mobile: homes, offices, parking lots, roadsides. It’s varied, it’s physical, and no two days match
