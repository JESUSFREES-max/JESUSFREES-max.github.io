Free Car Repair Manuals & Car Resources

Looking for a free repair manual for your car or truck? The tools on this page are free to use — library resources, government sites and community manuals, no signup scams. One card below (Scribd) is a subscription service, and it is labeled that way honestly. We put this together for DIYers around Clackamas, Beaverton, Gresham & Vancouver. And when a job turns out bigger than the driveway, we come to you.

Start Here — Safety First
🛎️ NHTSA Recall Check

Enter your VIN or license plate on the government’s site to see unrepaired safety recalls on your vehicle. Recall repairs are FREE at any dealer — every owner should check once a year.

Check My Recalls — Free

Full Repair Manuals
📖 Chilton Library — Free With a Library Card

The real professional manual: step-by-step repair procedures, wiring diagrams, and service bulletins. Free online with a Multnomah County Library card — and Clackamas County residents can get that card free. On the Vancouver side, Fort Vancouver Regional Libraries carries it too.

Chilton — Multnomah CountyChilton — Fort Vancouver

🔧 Operation CHARM — Free Factory Manuals Online

Factory service manuals you can read right in your browser, no signup. Strongest on vehicles through about 2013 — great for older cars and trucks.

Open Operation CHARM — Free

🍋 LEMON Manuals — For Newer Cars (Through 2025)

From the same project as Operation CHARM, covering newer models CHARM doesn’t have — manuals from 1960 through 2025. Also free; the site can run slow at times.

Open LEMON Manuals — Free

🛠️ iFixit — Free Step-by-Step Repair Guides

Photo-by-photo teardown and repair guides written by real people — great when you want to see every step. Their car & truck section is free.

Open iFixit Car & Truck Guides — Free

Your Official Owner’s Manual — Free From the Maker

Every maker posts owner’s manuals free on its own site. Pick your brand, choose year & model:

Ford Chevrolet / GM Toyota Honda Nissan Ram / Jeep / Dodge

📚 ManualsLib

A huge free library of manuals — a good fallback for older vehicles the makers no longer post.

Search ManualsLib — Free

Look It Up Before You Spend
🔎 NHTSA VIN Decoder

Free build and spec information straight from the government database — check it before buying parts or a used car.

Decode My VIN — Free

📋 YOUCANIC — Free VIN Report With Service Bulletins

One free lookup pulls your recalls, complaints, and the maker’s technical service bulletins (TSBs) for your VIN — the same bulletins dealers check first. No signup; a daily lookup limit applies.

Get My Free VIN Report

💡 OBD-Codes.com — Check-Engine Light Codes

Got a code like P0300 or P0171? Look up what it actually means — symptoms, likely causes, and common fixes, in plain English. Free.

Look Up My Code — Free

📣 CarComplaints.com — What Breaks on Your Model

Real owner complaints, problem patterns, recalls and investigations by year, make and model — check it before you buy a used car, or to see if your problem is a known one. Free.

Check My Model — Free

🔀 Car-Part.com — Which Other Cars Use Your Part?

The interchange search the salvage yards use: enter your car and the part you need, and it shows every other vehicle that shares that same part, at thousands of salvage yards across the country. A good used part is often the smart buy on an older car — and it widens your search way past your own model. Free, no signup. Pulling parts yourself? Ask us about doing it safely — and if you’d rather not install it yourself, we install customer-supplied parts.

Search Used Parts & Interchange — Free

One More Big Library — With an Honest Catch
📄 Scribd — Huge Manual Collection

Scribd hosts a very large collection of service manuals uploaded by its community. Honest note: Scribd is a subscription service — many documents can be read free online, but downloads and full access need a paid subscription, and upload quality varies. We include it because the collection is huge, but it is not one of the fully-free sources above.
